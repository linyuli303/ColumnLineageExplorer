      About this demo & lineage scope

      Solid edges represent declared value dependencies. Control mode adds selected join-key and grouping dependencies. This is a curated demonstration, not exhaustive query semantics: the quantity filter's row-membership effects are described at the frame level, not expanded to every column. Other inputs to a derived column are not ancestors of the selected input itself.

      All names, transformations, and mappings are synthetic. Lineage is explicitly declared, not inferred from row counts or automatically extracted from arbitrary Python. Runs entirely in your browser; no external libraries or data uploads.
